Common Questions

GOT
QUESTIONS?

Do you actually create the content, or just tell me what to post?+

We create it. That's the entire point of Ampvox. Strategy, scripting, filming direction, editing, scheduling and posting are all handled. Content production runs through vetted specialists we brief and manage. You approve a monthly plan and then get on with running your business.

Who do you work best with?+

Businesses that know social and automation matter but have no time or appetite to do it themselves. Typically B2C brands, service businesses and founder-led companies. And brands who want to run creator campaigns without managing five influencers across five inboxes.

Do you guarantee results?+

No, and be sceptical of anyone who does. Social platforms change their algorithms without warning and no agency controls that. What we do commit to is the work: the content gets made, the systems get built, the deals get contracted, and you see honest reporting on what happened either way.

How does the AI side work, and do I need to understand it?+

No. We scope it, build it, test it and hand it over working. You get documentation so you're never locked in, and 30 days of tuning after launch. If you'd rather understand the internals we'll walk you through them, but nothing requires you to.

What does working with your creators involve?+

You brief us, we match you to the right creators, and we handle the rest: contracts, usage rights, ASA disclosure compliance and delivery. You deal with one person. Creators on the roster are managed on commission, so there are no retainers on either side.

How do you charge?+

Fixed price against a written scope, agreed before anything starts. Managed social is £1,750 a month with a three-month minimum. Builds and sprints are one-off fees, split into a 50% deposit and the balance on delivery. Ad spend, software licences and API costs are billed at cost on top, always with your approval first. Creator management is commission only, and brands pay no fee to us at all.
